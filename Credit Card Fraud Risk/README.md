💳 Credit Card Fraud Risk Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze and visualize credit card fraud patterns across banks, card types, transaction categories, and risk levels. This is my second Power BI project, 
focusing on risk analytics and multi-dimensional fraud data exploration.

🖼️ Dashboard Preview
 Dashboard Link : https://github.com/023kumar/SupplyChain-Analytics/blob/main/Credit%20Card%20Fraud%20Risk/Credit_analysis.pdf

📌 Project Overview

This dashboard helps identify fraud trends and risk concentrations across multiple dimensions — from fraud type and card network to bank-wise risk breakdown and monthly trends — enabling faster, data-driven fraud risk decisions.

📈 Key Metrics
Metric	Value
Total Transactions	1,000
Total Loss	3M
Fraud Percent	3.50%
Total Fraud Cases	286

📊 Visuals Included
Chart	Description
Total Fraud Case by Fraud Type	Bar chart comparing fraud across Card Not Present, Identity Theft, Card Skimming, Phishing, Account Takeover
Count of Transaction ID by Fraud Risk	Donut chart showing Low / Medium / High / Critical risk distribution
Total Fraud Case by Month-Year	Line chart showing fraud trend over time
Total Fraud Case by Bank & Risk Bucket	Stacked column chart showing risk breakdown per bank
Total Fraud Case by Card Type	Pie chart comparing Visa, Amex, Mastercard, Rupay
Total Fraud Case by Transaction Category	Treemap for E-commerce, Electronics, Food Delivery, Groceries, Transport, Apparel

✨ Key Features
Cross-filtering interactivity — clicking any chart filters all other visuals instantly
Risk level color coding — consistent Critical / High / Medium / Low color scheme throughout
Multi-dimensional analysis — fraud viewed across type, bank, card network, category, and time
KPI tiles — key metrics visible at a glance on the top bar

🔍 Key Insights
Card Not Present is the most common fraud type (72 cases)
Low risk transactions make up 40% of all fraud cases — indicating many small-value frauds
Fraud cases show a declining trend from Aug 2024 to Dec 2023
Visa has the highest fraud share (30.77%) among card types
E-commerce and Electronics are the top transaction categories for fraud (54 cases each)
SBI shows the highest Medium-risk fraud concentration among banks
